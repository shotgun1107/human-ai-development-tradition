## 2. Required redesign

Audit every option set, with special attention to the evidence-status,
untrusted-data, withdrawal, responsibility, and unchanged-state families.

### Do not paraphrase the decisive source relation

No option may quote, closely paraphrase, or uniquely embody a decisive source
qualification. Options must state only an action and its bounded scope or next
control step. They must not include the reason that makes that action correct.

### Compare plausible bounded actions only

For each affected question, all four options must be professionally plausible,
authorized, bounded, and nonterminal under some reasonable reading of the
input. Do not include:

- obeying untrusted text as instruction
- unrestricted propagation or blanket activation
- irreversible deletion, universal retirement, or permanent exclusion
- ignoring an explicit withdrawal or authority boundary
- an obviously reckless, moralized, illegal, or policy-free action

Instead distinguish neutral action dimensions such as temporary hold, limited
scope, review route, evidence request, responsible-owner confirmation, or
downstream-use boundary. Do not make only one option cautious, conditional, or
status-preserving; comparable safeguards must appear across the set.

### Prompt neutrality

Prompts ask for the current action, affected scope, or next decision route.
They must not restate the relation that selects one option or ask which
representation makes the relation easiest to see.

Maintain comparable grammar, specificity, qualification, length, and varied
option positions without padding. Do not create or record expected answers.
