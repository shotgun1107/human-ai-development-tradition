## 2. Finding dispositions

### M-04 - unique failure routing

The v0.3 successor fixes one ordered reservation step for all four output
names. Initial collisions are `AR-RESERVE/0`; collisions appearing after
reservation are `AR-RECORD/1` and preserve the other actor's destination.

It adds an exact post-I1 `STUB_INTEGRITY` operation requiring counters
`1/1/1/1/0`; missing close, close failure, or any mismatch is
`AR-INVOKE/1`. It also orders semantic determinism audit before canonical
serialization and exact record comparison, so nondeterministic semantics are
`AR-DETERMINISM` and later byte/I/O failures are `AR-RECORD`.

**Proposed disposition:** closed at public design-text level.

### M-05 - Windows publication semantics

The unnamed abstract primitive is replaced by one bounded profile:
Windows 10/11, one local NTFS volume, direct `MoveFileExW`, numeric flags `0`,
no replace/copy/fallback/retry. Unsupported platforms fail preflight.

The claim is explicitly limited to process interruption and ordinary API
failure. Power-loss, kernel-failure, and post-return directory-metadata
durability are excluded. Future implementation review must exercise the real
primitive and verify its exact signature, flags, volume identity, collisions,
and source-code absence of fallback paths.

**Proposed disposition:** closed at public design-text level.

### M-06 - pending residue and consumer rule

The run record alone is no longer success evidence. Success requires the exact
pair `run-record.json` plus `publication-receipt.json`, both pending names
absent, exact bindings, and acceptance by
`S0-PUBLIC-ASSEMBLY-EVIDENCE-CONSUMER-1` under the authenticated output-root
identity.

Receipt publication is the sole commit point. A record left before that point,
copied pending bytes, a wrong pathname, or a cleanup-failed root is rejected.
Cleanup failure marks the root `QUARANTINED-FAILED` without changing the first
primary failure class.

**Proposed disposition:** closed at public design-text level.

### M-07 - implementation, design, review, and close provenance

**Proposed disposition:** closed at public design-text level.
