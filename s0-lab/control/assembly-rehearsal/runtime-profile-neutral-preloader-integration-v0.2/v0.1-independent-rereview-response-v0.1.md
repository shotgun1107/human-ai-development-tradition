## 3. Why preloader ownership is the minimum correction

Effective runtime/preloader v0.3 already freezes the preloader as the sole
parent-relative `NtCreateFile` caller and original root-handle holder. v0.4
retains that construction while requiring its complete profile and semantic
validation. Creating a new CONTROL-to-preloader retained-handle protocol would
add a second authority surface and depart from the effective predecessor.

The v0.2 revision therefore removes the conflicting integration state instead
of inventing a handoff. CONTROL supplies authenticated parent/leaf intent;
the preloader creates and continuously retains the sole original; the verifier
receives only the already frozen reduced same-object duplicate.

## 4. Why one generic terminal was insufficient

The existing assembly child reaches `COMPLETE` only after the exact second
publication reply, but effective assembly v0.5 says that state is diagnostic
only. Effective runtime/preloader authority separately requires an exact
publication witness, admission `COMMIT-ACKED`, and a first terminal consumer
attempt that enters `SPENT` on acceptance or `REJECTED` on failure.

The v0.2 outer machine now names each of those facts and their ordering. Its
positive terminal is `LIVE-CONSUMED`, not `COMPLETE` or generic `TERMINAL`.
Even `LIVE-CONSUMED` remains bounded same-process acceptance only and does not
open or claim the official rehearsal gate.
