## 1. Purpose and noncompensatory decision

This plan is designed to reject URC-0.7 before it can influence sealed work. It does not compare representation superiority and does not validate S0, a central system, a tradition system, or Project Brain integration.

The design may advance only if every P0-P4 gate passes under artifacts, role grants, schedules, thresholds, algorithms, grids, and ceilings frozen before confirmatory data. No gate compensates for another. A post-result change creates a new reviewed version and untouched bank.

## 7. P2 — no-record leakage and neutral-token equivalence

### 7.1 Topology and hypotheses

Two independently prompted observer identities O1 and O2 each run `original` and `neutral` variants. A stream receives runner-visible prompt, 24-row codebook, question projection, and structural contract, but no records, metadata, labels, row frequencies, other batches, outputs, or examples.

Each stream uses `K=N/12` fresh one-turn sessions. One session predicts an exact one-of-24 row for a frozen packet of 12 questions. Original and neutral variants use matched packets and separate sessions. Observers never see bank balance.

For stream `(o,v)`, `p[o,v]` is exact-row accuracy. `delta[o]=p[o,original]-p[o,neutral]`.

- Four leakage claims test `H0-L: p>=0.15` against `p<0.15`, each at one-sided alpha 0.00625.
- Two equivalence claims test `H0-E: delta<=-0.04 or delta>=+0.04` against strict interior equivalence, each at claim alpha 0.0125.

The Bonferroni total is `4*0.00625 + 2*0.0125 = 0.05`, limited to the audited grid.

### 7.2 Exact stochastic model

All random variables below are mutually independent except where an index is explicitly shared. For observer o, question q, variant v, and packet k containing q:

- `Q[o,q]`, `S[o,v,k]`, and `E[o,v,q]` are independent standard normal variables;
- Q is shared by original and neutral for the same observer/question;
- S is shared by the 12 questions in exactly one observer/variant packet and not across variants or packets;
- O1 and O2 have independent Q, S, and E families; and
- `L[o,v,q] = sqrt(rho_q)*Q[o,q] + sqrt(rho_s)*S[o,v,k] + sqrt(1-rho_q-rho_s)*E[o,v,q]`.

Correctness is 1 exactly when `L[o,v,q] <= PhiInverse(p[o,v])`. The dependence grid is `rho_q` in `{0.20,0.40,0.60}` crossed with `rho_s` in `{0.00,0.10,0.20}`; all nine sums are below one.

For equivalence midpoint m and difference delta, `p[original]=m+delta/2` and `p[neutral]=m-delta/2`. Null boundaries use delta `-0.04` and `+0.04` with m in `{0.10,0.15,0.20,0.30}`. Power uses delta 0 and m in `{0.09,0.11}`. Leakage-null streams set their one marginal to 0.15. These mappings are the only permitted conversion from grid parameters to probabilities.

### 7.3 Statistics, calibration, and power

For leakage, `x[k]` is the mean of 12 correctness values. For equivalence, `d[k]=x[original,k]-x[neutral,k]`. The standard error is sample standard deviation divided by square root K.

With positive constants `cL(N)` and `cE(N)`:

- leakage passes only if `(mean(x)-0.15)/SE(x) < -cL(N)`;
- equivalence passes only if `(mean(d)+0.04)/SE(d) > cE(N)` and `(mean(d)-0.04)/SE(d) < -cE(N)`.

With zero SE, the constant mean must be strictly below 0.15 or the constant difference strictly inside `(-0.04,+0.04)`. No confirmatory resampling occurs.

For every N, each null-grid point receives 100,000 fit datasets. Constants are restricted to `0.0000, 0.0001, ..., 8.0000`; select the smallest whose fit false-pass rate is at most half the allocated claim alpha at every relevant point. A separate 100,000-dataset audit uses the selected constant unchanged. The one-sided 99.9% Clopper-Pearson upper bound must not exceed the allocated claim alpha at any point.

After calibration, joint power for all six claims is simulated at delta 0, p 0.09 and 0.11, and every dependence point. Passing uses 100,000 datasets and a one-sided 99% Clopper-Pearson lower bound of at least 0.80 at every point. Early stop is allowed only when the corresponding 99% upper bound is below 0.80, which fails N.

## 9. P3 — representation-alignment equivalence

### 9.1 Control and topology

The control has one decision field `outcome_code` accepting equal-length `K01` through `K24`. A post-bank Fisher-Yates permutation maps codes to rows. The codebook states all meanings in code order without examples, frequencies, recommendations, or condition language.

Control and URC are otherwise identical in citations, affected IDs, uncertainty, reason, canonicalization, model, authority, tools, opportunities, and limits. Each grammar crosses A-D representations. `K=N/8` matched packets contain eight questions; each packet has eight fresh sessions, one per grammar-representation cell.

### 9.2 Estimator and question-level outcomes

`Y[q,g,r]` follows the candidate's level table. Classes before parseable JSON invalidate the suite. A response envelope/membership defect makes all eight Y values in that response zero. An answer-local schema defect makes only that question zero. Otherwise Y is one only when every semantic atom for the question is true.

For each grammar, the representation gap is the all-question mean of `(Y[C]+Y[D]-Y[A]-Y[B])/2`. `theta` is the URC gap minus control gap. Packet contrast `z[k]` applies the formula within one matched packet; `SE(z)` is packet sample standard deviation divided by square root K.

P3 passes only when `(mean(z)+0.10)/SE(z) > c3(N)` and `(mean(z)-0.10)/SE(z) < -c3(N)`. With zero SE, the constant contrast must be strictly inside `(-0.10,+0.10)`.

### 9.3 Complete stochastic model and valid null grid

For question q, cell c among the eight grammar-representation cells, and packet k:

- `Q[q]`, `S[c,k]`, and `E[c,q]` are mutually independent standard normals;
- Q is shared across all eight cells for the same question;
- S is shared by the eight questions in exactly one cell/packet session; and
- residuals are independent over cell and question.

`L[c,q]=sqrt(rho_q)*Q[q]+sqrt(rho_s)*S[c,k]+sqrt(1-rho_q-rho_s)*E[c,q]`, and correctness is 1 iff `L[c,q] <= PhiInverse(p[c])`.

The dependence grid is `rho_q` in `{0.10,0.30,0.50}` crossed with `rho_s` in `{0.00,0.10,0.20}`. The base grid is the six explicit pairs `(p_low,gap)`:

`(0.55,0.00)`, `(0.55,0.10)`, `(0.65,0.00)`, `(0.65,0.10)`, `(0.75,0.00)`, `(0.75,0.10)`.

For every pair, control A/B are `p_low`, control C/D are `p_low+gap`, URC A/B are `p_low`, and URC C/D are `p_low+gap+theta`. Null calibration uses theta `-0.10` and `+0.10`. All resulting probabilities lie in `[0.45,0.95]`; no point is excluded or undefined. Power uses theta 0.

Each N uses 100,000 fit datasets, the fixed 0.0001 constant grid, an independent 100,000 audit, the half-alpha fit rule, and a 99.9% Clopper-Pearson audit upper bound at claim alpha 0.05. Power uses 100,000 datasets per point and a 99% lower bound of at least 0.80. Claims remain limited to this grid.

## 10. Deterministic N and guaranteed-balanced schedule

### 10.1 Smallest sufficient N

Evaluate N in ascending order. N qualifies only if every P2/P3 null audit and every P2 joint/P3 power point passes. Select the first qualifying N. If none through 720 qualifies, authoring does not start. No grid, margin, alpha, formula, constant, or threshold changes without a new reviewed version.

### 10.2 P2 schedule

P2 cells are O1-original, O1-neutral, O2-original, O2-neutral. Packet k rotates the base order left by `k mod 4`; positions 1-2 and 3-4 are sequential waves of two. Every K=N/12 is divisible by four, so every cell occupies every serial position and wave equally.

### 10.3 P3 schedule construction and proof

P3 base cells remain `URC-A`, `CONTROL-C`, `URC-B`, `CONTROL-D`, `URC-C`, `CONTROL-A`, `URC-D`, `CONTROL-B`. Positions 1-3, 4-6, and 7-8 form sequential waves of three, three, and two.

Write `K=8q+r`. For every authorized N, r is exactly 2 or 6. Give each of the eight rotations q copies. Derive `s` uniformly from `{0,1,2,3}` from the bank-hash schedule seed:

- if r=2, add rotations `s` and `(s+4) mod 8` once;
- if r=6, add each rotation except `s` and `(s+4) mod 8` once.

Then use a separate bank-hash-seeded Fisher-Yates permutation to assign this fixed rotation multiset to packet order. The seed changes order and s, never the balance property.

Proof obligation: full cycles contribute equally. The two rotations separated by four place their three-position wave-1 sets disjointly, their three-position wave-2 sets disjointly, and their two-position wave-3 sets disjointly; each cell therefore receives zero or one residual placement per wave. The six-rotation case is their complement and also differs by at most one. Distinct residual rotations give each cell zero or one residual count in each serial position. Thus every authorized K and seed outcome guarantees per-position and per-wave count differences at most one. The checker verifies this theorem's counts before calls but cannot reject a seed and redraw.

A wave completes before the next begins; concurrency never exceeds three. Packet order and all timestamps freeze in the schedule manifest.

### 10.4 Revision, load, and drift

The suite does not start without an immutable exact model revision or fingerprint shared by every cell. Reasoning level, tier, region, tools/network, token limits, and timeout freeze.

For each session the dispatcher records scheduled wave, monotonic request-send time, optional provider-start and first-token diagnostics, monotonic final-byte receipt time, revision, tier, region, throttling, errors, and raw hash before semantics are opened.

Any revision, reasoning, tier, region, authority, tool-state difference, provider retry, rate-limit retry, undeclared routing, overlap across waves, or execution window longer than seven calendar days invalidates the suite. Latency alone is retained for P4 and never permits selective rerun.

## 12. P4 — comparator reliability and burden

### 12.1 Output consequences

P4 response schema success is one only when the top-level envelope, membership, and every answer/field format atom are true. It is zero for any parseable response or answer schema defect. Pre-parse classes are diagnostic zero but invalidate the suite, so P4 cannot pass independently. P3 uses the separate question rules in section 9.2. No class is imputed.

### 12.2 Comparator reliability

Both frozen comparators run the mutation catalog 100 times. All 200 runs must be byte-identical on format atoms, availability, surface-to-cause assignments, source/prior/residual/consumed sets, events, diagnostics, and totals. Semantic judges must agree on every reason mutation and confirmatory reason. Any difference fails P1/P4; CONTROL cannot select a view.

### 12.3 Exact burden metrics

For each first P3 response:

- `elapsed_ms = (monotonic timestamp immediately after final raw response byte is durably received) - (monotonic timestamp immediately before the first request byte is sent)`;
- the interval includes local send, provider queue, model generation, network return, and durable first-output write;
- provider-start and first-token intervals are diagnostics only;
- a missing/nonmonotonic endpoint invalidates the suite; and
- raw bytes are the original response-file bytes before parsing.

Pooling includes all first outputs in the four URC cells or four control cells. Median sorts values ascending; for even count it is the arithmetic mean of the two central values. Define time burden as `median_URC_elapsed / median_CONTROL_elapsed - 1` and byte burden analogously. A zero control median is invalid.

Every condition must hold:

- URC response schema success at least 0.95;
- URC minus control schema success at least -0.03;
- max-minus-min URC schema success across A-D at most 0.05;
- time burden at most 0.20;
- byte burden at most 0.20;
- all canonical/raw size and four-ID cardinality limits hold deterministically.

The matched P3 packet is the only resampling unit. For the five statistical thresholds, use 100,000 packet-cluster percentile-bootstrap replicates with the post-bank metric-specific seed. Resample K packets with replacement and retain all eight cell responses in each sampled packet. Quantiles use one-based rank `ceil(q*100000)`, clamped to 1..100000.

Passing requires: the lower 95% interval endpoint at least 0.95 for pooled schema; lower endpoint at least -0.03 for schema difference; upper endpoint at most 0.05 for A-D range; and upper endpoint at most 0.20 for each burden ratio. P4 cannot rescue P0-P3.
