## 5. F-01 blocking review: quality blindness and relevance separation

All answers below must be yes.

### 5.1 People and exposure

- Are Q1 and Q2 distinct natural people from each other, both leakage auditors and every role previously exposed to labeled D/B, task/oracle meaning, condition identity or relevance identity?
- Are Q1 and Q2 prohibited from every other E0 role?
- Does an append-only person exposure ledger plus access log, not a label change or declaration alone, determine eligibility?
- Are accounts, agents, sessions and worktrees explicitly unable to create human independence?
- Do Q1 and Q2 remain unable to receive leakage findings, relevance information, task/oracle meaning, mapping or each other's record?

Any path allowing a previously exposed person to become Q1/Q2 is blocking `FAIL`.

### 5.2 Lock chronology

- Are D/B closed and hashed before review?
- Are randomized X/Y and topic-stripped X/Y copies made under a frozen rule with mapping withheld from Q reviewers?
- Does each Q reviewer independently lock every semantic parity item, internal quality, authority, specificity, actionability, completeness and general-guidance judgment?
- Does each then lock a topic-stripped forced condition guess, confidence and reasons without receiving new information?
- Are all Q records timestamped and hashed before any relevance information is released or any relevance audit starts?
- Do missing locks, contact, cross-review, access or chronology error reject the package?

If quality can be amended after relevance begins, F-01 is `FAIL`.

### 5.3 Separate fresh relevance audit

- Are R1 and R2 different fresh natural people from Q1/Q2, leakage auditors, authors and all mapping/execution/adjudication/analysis roles?
- Do they receive only the minimum semantic relevance information after Q lock receipt?
- Are B irrelevance and D's prespecified applicable/non-applicable/stale/withdrawn relation judged for every family independently by both?
- Are Q records and the other R review hidden from each R reviewer?
- Does any `FAIL`, `UNCLEAR`, disagreement, missing item or identity/chronology breach reject the package without a resolver?

If relevance is performed by a Q reviewer, leakage auditor or another previously involved person, F-01 is `FAIL`.

### 5.4 Cue decision and staffing consequences

- Does a correct topic-stripped D guess with an authority/specificity/actionability/completeness/general-guidance imbalance reason fail the package?
- Has confirmatory staffing risen from 14 to at least 18 natural people?
- Are Q1, Q2, R1 and R2 separately represented in compensation, active/wait/calendar time, replacement, AI-support call, token and cash maxima?
- Do V3-G2, G4 and G6 enforce those requirements?

If the old combined leakage/B-review path remains possible, F-01 is `FAIL` and the package cannot pass.

## 6. F-02 major review: family-set counts and vector power

### 6.1 Set identity and overlap

- Are `F_R` and `F_H` named and frozen before material authoring?
- Are they disjoint in v0.3, with dual membership prohibited?
- Is `N_union` explicitly workload-only?
- Do pilot, calibration, reserve and optional diagnostic families count zero toward confirmatory sets until a prespecified valid reserve release?
- Does any endpoint not scorable on the whole parent set require its own named subset, membership, minimum and power term?

An undefined overlap or a single total N is major `FAIL`.

### 6.2 Every estimand and guardrail

Confirm the plan separately records membership, valid count, absolute floor and endpoint-specific power for:

1. D−A acceptable effect;
2. D−B acceptable effect;
3. D−A nonacceptable-risk harm;
4. D−B nonacceptable-risk harm;
5. D−A harm composite;
6. D−B harm composite;
7. relevant-stratum condition guessing;
8. harm-stratum condition guessing.

Every row must have at least 20 valid independent families and meet its own power requirement. Shared membership may imply equal numeric counts but may not erase a row. Critical must be a zero-count whole-run stop over the complete planned exposure, and operating ceilings must cover every planned and reserve call.

If 20 combined families can be split into underpowered sets and still pass, F-02 is `FAIL`.

### 6.3 Selection, allocation and power

- Are separate selection frames, IDs, instance counts, execution order and reserve order frozen for `F_R` and `F_H` before authoring?
- Can no result move or replace a family across sets?
- Do all A/B/D conditions use fresh sessions on every selected instance?
- Are repeats and instances prohibited from increasing family N?
- Does the future calculation output eight endpoint-specific requirements and then separate `N_relevant_required` and `N_harm_required` maxima?
- Is joint planning performed on `(N_relevant, N_harm)`, with nuisance inputs, multiplicity, validity loss, output vector and joint-pass probability frozen and hashed?
- Can no surplus in one set cover a deficit in the other?

The reviewer does not run power. It reviews whether the future calculation is uniquely specified enough to stop.

### 6.4 Cost propagation

- Do workload, executor calls, retry and reserve calls, tokens, people time and cash retain separate relevant and harm terms before totals?
- Does the worksheet carry all eight counts and both required set sizes?
- Does failure to fit either set within the approved ceiling cause `STOP/DEFER` rather than reduced clusters or guardrails?

If cost can be approved from a scalar N, F-02 is `FAIL`.

## 8. Preserved v0.2 correction review

### 8.1 Single representation

Exactly one active, versioned, owned, hashed and approved `selected_representation_ref` must exist before authoring. Fallback must receive identical authentication. Empty, plural, inline, asymmetric or changed references must stop or invalidate dependent work.

### 8.2 Estimand and worthwhile-effect alignment

The primary effect must remain equal-family-weighted. Nulls must remain `δ ≤ Δ`, effect pass must require cluster lower bounds `> Δ`, planning alternatives must exceed the same Δ, and pilot results must not lower margins or counts. A lower bound above zero alone cannot pass.

### 8.3 Transfer versus near-copy

Latent pattern must freeze first; curator and task/oracle author remain separated; token overlap, one-to-one skeleton, chronology and self-containment fail closed; four-of-six axes, two new decisive relations and one unusable source relation remain required; both leakage scopes must pass; oracle access remains outside repository and executor-visible environments.

### 8.4 Limited safety unblinding

Adjudicators remain condition-blind. Only the safety monitor may open one label after blind critical lock. A confirmed critical stops the whole run regardless of label; no favorable subset or automatic non-D restart is allowed.

### 8.5 Redaction and adjudication blinding

Raw output is independently redacted and QC'd without deleting decision meaning. Severity locks before guess. Mapping arrives after all locks. Explicit cue, missing guess, asymmetric or meaning-damaging redaction, or either family-stratum guess failure must prohibit pass.

### 8.6 System boundary and claim ceiling

S0/E0 separation, authenticated interface without winner narrative, external hidden oracle, worktree limitation, rollback, noncombination of contaminated results and the restricted claim ladder must remain explicit. No central-system, Project Brain, operational, economic, real-project, fine-tuning or model-transfer claim is allowed.

Any regression in these passed items is at least major and cannot be excused as outside F-01/F-02/F-03.
