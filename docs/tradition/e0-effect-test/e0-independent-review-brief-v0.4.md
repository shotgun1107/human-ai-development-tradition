## 5. F-01 blocking review: durable Q blindness across versions

All answers below must be yes.

### 5.1 Lifetime semantic exposure

- Does `CAND_DB` cover every full and topic-stripped D/B-derived candidate, including randomized unmapped X/Y, rejected packages, stable candidates and superseded versions?
- Is exposure recorded when access begins and retained for the E0 lifetime?
- Does candidate exposure permanently bar that natural person from Q1/Q2 on every later package version, repair, amendment or restart?
- Are deleted files, new labels, accounts, sessions, agents, employers or worktrees unable to clear exposure?
- Are `L_DB`, `T_ORACLE`, `COND_ID` and `REL_ID` still independently recorded?

Any path where prior candidate semantics can be seen and the person later serves as Q is blocking `FAIL`.

### 5.2 Fail-closed natural-person identity

- Is each person resolved to one stable natural-person ID with all known aliases before roster lock?
- Must identity evidence, lifetime ledger and access logs agree?
- Do uncertain identity, possible duplicates, unlinked aliases, missing access history or inability to prove a clean history make the person ineligible?
- Are declarations alone insufficient?

Treating identity uncertainty as no exposure is blocking `FAIL`.

### 5.3 First-cycle lock chronology

- Are D/B closed and hashed before review?
- Are randomized X/Y and topic-stripped X/Y copies made under a frozen rule with mapping withheld from Q reviewers?
- Does each Q independently lock every semantic parity and internal-quality item before a topic-stripped forced guess, confidence and reasons?
- Are all Q records timestamped and hashed before relevance release?
- Do missing locks, contact, cross-review, access or chronology error reject the package?
- Are two different fresh R people used only after complete Q locks, with no Q records or each other's records?

If quality can be amended after relevance begins, F-01 is `FAIL`.

### 5.4 Pair retirement and new-version restart

- After either Q accesses candidate bytes, do rejection, amendment, candidate change, access failure, missing/corrupt lock, withdrawal and attempted replacement retire both Qs?
- Does every allowed next attempt require a new package version, new randomized and stripped copies, new mapping/locks and two new clean natural people?
- Are old Q judgments, guesses, reasons and partial records prohibited from reuse?
- Are retired Qs prohibited from seeing or comparing later candidates?
- Is a stable D/B explicitly not an exception?
- Is zero-access correction allowed only when access logs positively prove neither Q received candidate bytes?

Replacing only one Q, rerandomizing labels or reusing an unmodified D/B with an old reviewer is blocking `FAIL`.

### 5.5 Staffing and resource consequences

- Does the first untouched confirmation cycle still require at least 18 distinct natural people?
- Is `J_Q_restart_max` frozen before access and are two additional prequalified people required for every permitted restart?
- Are identity review, availability, absence, compensation, active/wait/calendar time, AI support, human verification, tokens and cash represented for every fresh pair?
- Do V4-G2, G4 and G6 enforce those consequences?
- Does exhausted or unavailable fresh-pair coverage cause `STOP/DEFER`?

An unbudgeted fresh pair or a generic replacement reserve is F-01 `FAIL`.

- Are shared protocol, package/Q review and independent-review rows visible separately?

- Do V4-G2 and V4-G6 reject scalar-only human time or cash approval?
- Does inability to fit either family set, either reserve or fixed overhead cause `STOP/DEFER` rather than fewer clusters or weaker guardrails?
